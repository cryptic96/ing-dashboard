## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): Every transaction is correctly categorised: automatically by rules where they are confident, and by Claude and the user for the rest. Every change is audited and reversible, and the household can see where the money goes and how that compares with before.

The ROADMAP goal line is not written as a user story, so none is invented here.

This plan's slice: the operator sees the review queue shrink on /metrics and is alerted when writes hit their limit, hostile bank text arrives or categorisation fails.

<objective>
Expose the review-queue size (OPS-08) and the proposals waiting on /metrics with the refresher pattern (D-10: the backlog is worked down visibly), and add operator alerts without financial detail for write-limit refusals (D-22), suspicious bank text (D-24) and categorisation failures; document them.

Purpose: OPS-08 and the operator side of ADV-07's monitoring.
Output: ReviewMetrics, ReviewMetricsRefresher, three alert rules with their expression tests, docs/monitoring.md.
</objective>
