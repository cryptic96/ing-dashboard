## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): Every transaction is correctly categorised: automatically by rules where they are confident, and by Claude and the user for the rest. Every change is audited and reversible, and the household can see where the money goes and how that compares with before.

The ROADMAP goal line is not written as a user story, so none is invented here.

This plan's slice: after every sync, stored rules categorise new transactions; everything they cannot categorise confidently stays in the queue. Claude creates rules through MCP in plans 04-34 and 04-40; here rules are inserted directly by the tests.

Purpose: CAT-03 and the automatic half of CAT-05; the queue is everything this engine leaves unassigned.
Output: migration AddCategorisationRules, three entities, rule pass in CategorisationEngine, own accounts in the built-in pass, RuleEngineTests.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
