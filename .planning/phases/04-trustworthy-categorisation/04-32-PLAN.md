## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): Every transaction is correctly categorised: automatically by rules where they are confident, and by Claude and the user for the rest. Every change is audited and reversible, and the household can see where the money goes and how that compares with before.

The ROADMAP goal line is not written as a user story, so none is invented here.

This plan's slice: the user confirms a batch of Claude's proposals in one step; the queue shows what Claude proposed and why; and the backlog view lets Claude tackle the two years of uncategorised history by its biggest groups first.

<objective>
Show pending proposals in review_queue with Claude's reason (D-08), confirm a batch through the confirmation (D-21, D-09: confirmed proposals become user-sourced and certain), reject proposals, and add the backlog grouped by counterparty for the rules-first workflow (D-10); prove that reading every view writes nothing.

Purpose: CAT-06 (the user confirms) and CAT-05 (the queue is worked).
Output: proposals and backlog views, PreviewConfirmAsync and RejectAsync, ConfirmProposalsApplier, tests, docs/mcp.md queue paragraph.
</objective>
