## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): Every transaction is correctly categorised: automatically by rules where they are confident, and by Claude and the user for the rest. Every change is audited and reversible, and the household can see where the money goes and how that compares with before.

The ROADMAP goal line is not written as a user story, so none is invented here.

This plan's slice: Claude recognises that a payment-request service or a payment provider stands between the household and the real merchant and proposes flagging it; after the user confirms, its payments wait in the queue unless a rule has a second signal.

<objective>
Let Claude propose flagging and unflagging intermediaries (D-15) through the confirmation (D-21), as audited and undoable operations (D-25, D-26) that change no history, and list them in category_overview with their names as untrusted text.

Purpose: CAT-05 (payment requests and payments through payment service providers are not force-matched).
Output: SetIntermediaryAsync with IntermediaryAuditHandler, previews, Flag and Unflag appliers, change_ledger actions, overview section, IntermediaryTests.
</objective>
