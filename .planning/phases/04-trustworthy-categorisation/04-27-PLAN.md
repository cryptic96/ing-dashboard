## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): Every transaction is correctly categorised: automatically by rules where they are confident, and by Claude and the user for the rest. Every change is audited and reversible, and the household can see where the money goes and how that compares with before.

The ROADMAP goal line is not written as a user story, so none is invented here.

This plan's slice: Claude spots that a counterparty account is really the household's own (for example the unsynced savings account) and proposes it; after the user confirms, those transfers stop counting as spending, now and at every sync.

<objective>
Let Claude propose unsynced accounts as the household's own (D-17) through the confirmation (D-21), never overwriting user decisions (D-13), as audited and undoable operations (D-25, D-26), with account numbers kept on the server, and list them in category_overview.

Purpose: CAT-02 for accounts the bank connection cannot see.
Output: SetOwnAccountAsync with OwnAccountAuditHandler, previews in LedgerStructureService, Mark and Unmark appliers, change_ledger actions, overview section, OwnAccountTests.
</objective>
