## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): Every transaction is correctly categorised: automatically by rules where they are confident, and by Claude and the user for the rest. Every change is audited and reversible, and the household can see where the money goes and how that compares with before.

The ROADMAP goal line is not written as a user story, so none is invented here.

This plan's slice: Claude sees what is waiting for a category and why: no rule, an intermediary without a second signal, or conflicting rules. Proposals and the backlog view follow in plans 04-31 and 04-32.

<objective>
Expose the review queue (D-07: no rule, intermediary without a second signal, conflicting rules) through the read tool review_queue, with the reason recomputed per row by RuleMatcher, bank text wrapped as untrusted, the suspicious flag and the household note, and a stable keyset-paged order.

Purpose: CAT-05 (payment requests and payments through payment service providers go to a review queue instead of being force-matched).
Output: IReviewStore, ReviewStore, ReviewService, ReviewTools (review_queue), ReviewResults, ReviewQueueTests.
</objective>
