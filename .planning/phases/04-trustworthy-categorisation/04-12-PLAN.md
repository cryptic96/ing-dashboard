## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): Every transaction is correctly categorised: automatically by rules where they are confident, and by Claude and the user for the rest. Every change is audited and reversible, and the household can see where the money goes and how that compares with before.

The ROADMAP goal line is not written as a user story, so none is invented here.

This plan's slice: every total now excludes transfers because of their category's kind, so an account the household later marks as its own (plan 04-27) or a rule into Savings (plan 04-40) moves money out of spending the same way, and the upgrade leaves every figure the household already relies on unchanged.

<objective>
Switch the transfer exclusion in every read from the read-time account-number test to the category kind (D-16, D-06), backfill existing synced-account transfers in a migration of the same release, keep pending rows reported as pending, and keep partner flows counted (D-18). Prove the switch changes no existing total.

Purpose: CAT-02's exclusion half, with the upgrade risk (Pitfall 1) closed.
Output: migration AssignSyncedAccountTransfers, kind-based exclusion in LedgerQueryStore, seed helper running the transfer pass, upgrade test.
</objective>
