## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): After linking the ING accounts once, the household's real transactions arrive in the ledger every day. They are complete and never duplicated, bank-consent expiry never goes unnoticed, and both partners can see the data arriving.

This plan makes the data arrive by itself: after it, the ledger syncs every morning in Amsterdam time, retries once when that is safe, never burns the bank's call budget, records every failure, and the operator can ask for a sync now without risking the day's quota.

<objective>
Implement D-10 and D-11: the daily scheduler with one safe retry, the per-account call budget backed by an append-only call ledger, failure policy per outcome (including the consent-state flip on a consent rejection), restart safety, and the authenticated sync-now call with its last-call refusal. Operator-triggered syncs carry PSU context, the research's recommendation for Open Question 2 (reversible through Ingestion:PsuHeadersOnOperatorSyncs); it refines D-11 so a sync-now with the operator present spends no background quota, while the refusal rule still protects header-less calls.

Purpose: INGEST-01 (daily with no manual steps), INGEST-05 (the post-link sync keeps its longest-history fetch within budget), INGEST-07 (rate limits respected, every failure visible) and the sync-now part of API-01.
Output: Domain schedule decision and budget, provider-call ledger and migration, meter, scheduler, orchestrator failure policy, sync-now endpoint, tests and docs.
</objective>
