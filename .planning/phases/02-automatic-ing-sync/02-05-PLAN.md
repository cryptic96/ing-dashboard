## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): After linking the ING accounts once, the household's real transactions arrive in the ledger every day. They are complete and never duplicated, bank-consent expiry never goes unnoticed, and both partners can see the data arriving.

This plan makes the ledger trustworthy when the bank changes its mind: after it, a pending payment that books stays one transaction even when the bank changes its reference, ghosts disappear, and anything uncertain is shown as unclear instead of guessed.

<objective>
Complete the reconciliation algorithm of D-13 and D-14 on top of the tracer: certain-match merging of pending and booked transactions with different references, unclear-match flags, drops after complete fetches only, cancellations, restores, and forward-only status rules, all as pure Domain logic applied atomically by the store.

Purpose: INGEST-02 (a pending transaction that books ends up as one transaction; re-runs add nothing) for banks whose identifiers are not stable, which the research shows can happen.
Output: extended ReconciliationPlan and TransactionReconciler, store apply for merges, flags, drops and restores, orchestrator coverage, synthetic scenario helpers, unit and integration tests.
</objective>
