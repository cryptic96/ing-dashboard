## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): After linking the ING accounts once, the household's real transactions arrive in the ledger every day. They are complete and never duplicated, bank-consent expiry never goes unnoticed, and both partners can see the data arriving.

This plan makes sure nothing fails silently: after it, the operator gets an email when a sync fails, is rate limited or rejected, when data goes stale, when a balance does not reconcile, and 14 and 7 days before and on consent expiry.

<objective>
Expose the operational metrics of OPS-01 as a database projection with opaque labels, and provision the household alert rules of OPS-02 under D-17 (operator only, no financial text) and D-18 (transient failures after the retry or after 26 hours without success; rejections at once; consent at 14 and 7 days and on expiry; mail cap kept). Per the research's recommendation for Open Question 1, still-firing household alerts repeat once a day through a child route; this extends D-18's wording and is reversible by changing one repeat interval.

Purpose: OPS-01, OPS-02, INGEST-04 (warned at least 14 days ahead) and INGEST-07 (every failed sync visible).
Output: status read model, metrics and refresher, household rules, notification child route, lint and docs updates, tests.
</objective>
