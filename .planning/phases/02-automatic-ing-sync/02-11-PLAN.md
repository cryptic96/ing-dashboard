## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): After linking the ING accounts once, the household's real transactions arrive in the ledger every day. They are complete and never duplicated, bank-consent expiry never goes unnoticed, and both partners can see the data arriving.

This plan completes the partners' view: after it, the dashboard's top shows per account whether the data is arriving, how long the consent lasts, today's balance and whether it reconciles, and anything unclear.

<objective>
Finish D-16: add reporting.account_status and the status table at the top of the generated EN and NL dashboard. Operational state is read from reporting views rather than Prometheus (Claude's discretion under D-16) because the table must show each account's display name, which metric labels deliberately never carry.

Purpose: success criterion 4 (both partners see sync status and recent transactions per account, in English or Dutch, from one source, through the SELECT-only reporting role) and the partner-facing half of INGEST-04 (the partner sees consent state on the status row, per D-17).
Output: AddAccountStatusView migration, generator and translation changes, regenerated JSON, view and query tests.
</objective>
