## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): After linking the ING accounts once, the household's real transactions arrive in the ledger every day. They are complete and never duplicated, bank-consent expiry never goes unnoticed, and both partners can see the data arriving.

This plan moves the test suite to the new test platform before the many ingestion tests are written, and proves it with the first ingestion building block: the Amsterdam-time schedule math the daily sync runs on.

<objective>
Complete the folded test-platform todo: opt in to Microsoft.Testing.Platform, move both test projects to xunit.v3 4.x, add Microsoft.Extensions.TimeProvider.Testing, and switch the CI and release workflows to the new dotnet test syntax. Prove it with a real test-first slice: SyncSchedule's DST-safe conversions for the daily Europe/Amsterdam sync (D-10), filtered by trait under the new runner.

Purpose: every later plan in this phase writes its tests on this platform and uses --filter-trait in its verify commands; the schedule math is the base the scheduler builds on.
Output: global.json, both test csproj files and lock files, SyncSchedule plus tests, updated ci.yml and release.yml.
</objective>
