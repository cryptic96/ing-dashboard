## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): After linking the ING accounts once, the household's real transactions arrive in the ledger every day. They are complete and never duplicated, bank-consent expiry never goes unnoticed, and both partners can see the data arriving.

This plan connects the real bank: after it, the pipeline built on the synthetic provider runs unchanged against Enable Banking, and no request other than reading account information can ever leave the process.

<objective>
Implement the Enable Banking adapter behind IBankDataProvider (INGEST-06), shaped by the spike's recorded decisions (D-03): client-token minting, the pre-link application and ASPSP checks, consent start and code exchange, longest-history and incremental transaction paging, balances, revocation, error mapping, PSU headers on operator-triggered fetches, exact amount parsing, and the structural read-only guarantee of SEC-01 chosen under Claude's discretion (AIS-only interface, outbound allow-list, recorded-request test, no payment member).

Purpose: SEC-01, and the real-bank half of INGEST-01, INGEST-05 and INGEST-07.
Output: EnableBanking adapter files, MoneyParser, DI registration, the JWT package and lock files, unit and pipeline tests.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-13-01 | Elevation of Privilege | payment initiation through the aggregator | critical | mitigate | No payment member on the interface (reflection test); outbound allow-list of seven account-information routes on one https host (recorded-run and refusal tests); pre-link refusal when the application offers payment initiation; no payment route literal in production code (acceptance grep) |

| T-02-13-03 | Information Disclosure | tokens, session ids, codes or bank text in exceptions and logs | high | mitigate | Fixed exception messages; only validated error codes kept; no request URLs or bodies in messages; log-redaction sentinels extended in the runbook plan |

| T-02-13-06 | Denial of Service | oversized responses | low | mitigate | 16 MB response buffer limit and a 60-second timeout |
| T-02-13-07 | Tampering | spoofed authorisation URL | medium | mitigate | https plus aggregator host allow-list before the URL reaches the operator |
