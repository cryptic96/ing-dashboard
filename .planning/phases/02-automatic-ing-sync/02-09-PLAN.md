## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): After linking the ING accounts once, the household's real transactions arrive in the ledger every day. They are complete and never duplicated, bank-consent expiry never goes unnoticed, and both partners can see the data arriving.

This plan proves the ledger is complete: after it, every account has a daily balance snapshot and the ledger checks, to the cent, that the bank's balance equals the previous balance plus the booked transactions in between.

<objective>
Implement D-12: one balance fetch per account per day on the first successful sync, daily snapshots of every balance type, and exact reconciliation of the booked balance against the ledger's booked transactions, with any drift recorded for the metrics, alert and dashboard plans.

Purpose: success criterion "each account's transactions reconcile with the bank to the cent" (INGEST-02 completeness, INGEST-03 exact decimals); the snapshots also give later planning features real savings balances.
Output: BalanceReconciler, balance store and AddBalanceSnapshots migration, orchestrator balance step, tests.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions

- Reconciliation uses booked balance types with reference dates and booking-date windows; which types ING actually returns is a spike answer. If ING returns no booked type with a reference date, reconciliation stays unknown (visible on the dashboard) and the spike-completion plan decides whether Ingestion:ReconcileBalanceKinds changes.
- The research suggested treating a first-seen drift as unverified until the next day; the booking-date window avoids the race that motivated it, so drift is reported on first sight and the alert stays operator-only.
