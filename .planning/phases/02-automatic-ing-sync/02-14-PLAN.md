## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): After linking the ING accounts once, the household's real transactions arrive in the ledger every day. They are complete and never duplicated, bank-consent expiry never goes unnoticed, and both partners can see the data arriving.

This plan makes the real link safe to switch on: after it, real captured pairs have been replayed through the real code without a duplicate, the real data from the spike is gone, production refuses an incomplete bank configuration, and the operator has a runbook.

<objective>
Validate pending-to-booked reconciliation against the real captured pairs kept outside the repository (the roadmap's research flag) using the real adapter mapping and reconciler, then delete the captures (D-01). Finish production readiness for the Enable Banking provider: configuration validation, HttpClient log level, log-redaction sentinels through the adapter, the spike-chosen defaults, placeholder env lines and the operator runbook.

Purpose: success criterion 2 on real data shapes, and a safe, documented first real link for the go-live plan.
Output: replay test and in-memory ledger, validator and appsettings changes, redaction tests, defaults, env example, docs/bank-link.md, replay counts in 02-SPIKE.md.
</objective>
