## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): Claude can answer accurate "how much" and "what did we spend" questions from the household's real transactions, on desktop, in Claude Code and on claude.ai web and mobile, through a securely exposed MCP endpoint.

After this plan Claude can answer "how much did we spend at the supermarkets in August?" with one server-side total that states its dates, filters, count and exclusions (ROADMAP success criteria 2 and 3).

<objective>
Build money_totals as a vertical slice from the tool down to SQL: Amsterdam period resolution (OPS-06, D-11), booked-only sums with pending reported beside (D-09), own-account transfer exclusion (D-10), out, in and net per currency (D-12), all synced accounts by default with an optional per-account split (D-13), a per-counterparty breakdown because categories do not exist yet (D-08), and full provenance (ADV-02).

Purpose: the core value of the phase; every "how much" answer must be grounded and checkable.
Output: domain period, text and aggregation rules; store query; service shaping; the tool; unit and integration tests.
</objective>
