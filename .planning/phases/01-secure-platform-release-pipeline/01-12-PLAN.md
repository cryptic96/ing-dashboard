ROADMAP goal (verbatim): A tagged release deploys itself to the homelab LXC through a pipeline that cannot leak secrets. The running platform (app, PostgreSQL, Prometheus and Grafana) is locked down and ready to hold financial data before any real bank data arrives.

This plan proves the Walking Skeleton live: after it, a real tag on main has deployed itself to the real LXC through the gated, attested pipeline, and every success criterion of the phase has been observed.

Then observe the remaining success criteria on the real system: restart and redeploy survival, refusal of a modified artifact, a restore drill (D-18), LAN-only reachability, REST authentication and per-partner Grafana logins.
